How to Use Saleo Builder

Everything you need to know to create, customize, and deploy Saleo demos.

1

Create a New Saleo Demo

Click the "+ New Saleo Demo" button on the home page. Fill in the brand name and industry, and optionally add a brand description, website URL, brand colors, tone, and visual style. For the demo story, you have three options: Write Synopsis to type one yourself, Select Script to pull a synopsis from Scriptwriter, or Upload Script to extract one from a file. Whichever tab is active when you hit "Generate" will be used.

2

Apply a Brand Kit (Optional)

When creating or editing a demo, you can apply a Brand Kit from Brand Kit Builder. Click "Select Brand Kit" in the Brand Kit section, choose a kit from the dropdown, and it will auto-fill your brand name, description, website URL, industry, colors, tone, and visual style. You can also see which Brand Kit is linked on the detail page — click it to open in Brand Kit Builder.

3

Review Your Saleo Prompt

Once generated, you'll land on the demo detail page. The Saleo Prompt section contains the AI-generated prompt text — ready to copy and paste into the Saleo platform. You can click Copy to grab it or Edit to tweak it manually before using it.

4

Use the Prompt in Saleo

This app is designed for Saleo demos built off a Golden Demo. Open the token library in the Saleo extension by clicking the % icon, then click the "Saleo AI" button within the library. Paste in the Saleo Prompt from this page and you're good to go.

5

Download and Replace Images

Saleo Builder automatically generates 15 AI images for each demo: 4 brand images (cover, backgrounds, hero), 7 card images (lifestyle shots), and 4 product images. You can click any image to see a larger preview. Use the "⬇ Download All as ZIP" button to grab them all at once. In the Saleo console, click on each page, open its images tab, and click Replace Image — selecting the corresponding file from your download. You can also regenerate individual images or all at once using the "Generate All" button.

6

Refine with AI

Want to improve your demo prompt? In the View Settings panel on the demo detail page, use the "More info to further refine the Saleo demo" text box to add instructions — for example, "Focus more on loyalty program features" or "Make the language more enterprise-focused." Then click "✨ Regenerate Saleo Demo" and AI will rebuild the prompt with your guidance.

7

Update Demo Settings

On any demo's detail page, the View Settings panel shows all the fields you entered when creating the demo — brand name, description, website URL, industry, colors, and demo story synopsis. You can also attach or change a Brand Kit or Scriptwriter script from here. Change any field anytime — a yellow "You have unsaved changes" banner will appear with a green Save button so you never lose track of what's changed.

8

Share with Teammates

Click the share icon on any demo to send a copy to another user by email. They'll get their own editable copy of the full Saleo demo in their account. If you share to the same person again, you can choose to replace their previous copy or send them a new one.
